Frequently Asked Questions

No pictures are stored on any server. All processing, background segmentation, and canvas scaling happen directly inside your web browser.
While this app formats your photos according to official parameters (such as Singapore ICA's 400x514px and head height guidelines), final approval is strictly subject to the authority of each country's passport office.
The web app leverages client-side WebAssembly and machine learning algorithms (`@imgly/background-removal`) to isolate the person from the background image right inside your phone or desktop browser.
